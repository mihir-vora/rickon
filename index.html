Three decades of moving oil

From a single workshop in Odhav to over a hundred pump and motor models trusted by crusher plants, CNC shops and oil handling units across India.

Rickon Engineering was established in 1989. We manufacture rotary gear pumps, hydraulic pumps, crusher oil pumps, electric motors, stone crusher spare parts, industrial vibrators, coolant pumps and SS gear pumps. Under the guidance of our CEO, Mr. Babubhai Chodvadiya, every pump is assembled and tested in-house before dispatch, and customers are always welcome to visit the factory.
